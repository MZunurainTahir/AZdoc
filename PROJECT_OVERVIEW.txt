================================================================================
AZDOC: AI HEALTH & DIAGNOSTICS FOR EVERY LIVING SYSTEM
Comprehensive Project Overview, Problem Domain, and Target Audience
================================================================================

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CORE MISSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AZdoc is an all-in-one, multi-domain AI healthcare and diagnostic ecosystem built 
to bridge the accessibility gap between advanced clinical/veterinary intelligence 
and underserved populations in Pakistan and developing regions. 

By unifying human healthcare, livestock veterinary advice, domestic pet wellness, 
indoor plant care, and agricultural crop management into a single digital platform, 
AZdoc democratizes expert-level diagnostics, multilingual voice consultations, and 
rapid local supply chain fulfillment.


2. THE PROBLEMS AZDOC SOLVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Traditional healthcare and agricultural systems face severe structural bottlenecks 
across developing regions:
* Rural Medical Deserts: Limited physical access to qualified human doctors, 
  veterinarians, and agronomists in remote villages and rural towns.
* Language and Literacy Barriers: Complex medical and agricultural terminology 
  published primarily in English, excluding millions of farmers, livestock owners, 
  and daily wage earners.
* Supply Chain Friction: Disconnection between disease diagnosis and immediate access 
  to authentic medicines, veterinary drugs, or agricultural fertilizers.
* Fragmented Ecosystems: Separate, disconnected apps for human health, pet care, 
  and crop disease monitoring, creating high friction for rural households that 
  manage all three simultaneously.


3. TARGET AUDIENCE & STAKEHOLDERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AZdoc is engineered for a diverse spectrum of users across five distinct domains:
* Humans & Patients: Individuals seeking rapid clinical triage, skin condition 
  analysis, symptom checking, and local pharmacy delivery.
* Livestock Farmers & Herders: Rural communities requiring instant diagnosis and 
  treatment plans for cattle, goats, poultry, and farm animals.
* Domestic Pet Owners: Urban and suburban pet owners looking for reliable wellness 
  guidance for cats, dogs, and companion animals.
* Plant & Crop Growers: Farmers and agricultural workers monitoring crop leaf 
  diseases, soil health, and regional weather outbreak risks.
* Local Pharmacists & Retailers: Partner vendors connected via live GPS radar 
  to fulfill prescriptions and deliver supplies via Cash-on-Delivery (COD).


4. KEY INNOVATIONS & TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Multilingual Voice AI: Native regional support including Urdu (Nastaliq), 
  Punjabi, Sindhi, Pashto, Balochi, and Spanish powered by Speechmatics, 
  Groq Whisper, and ElevenLabs TTS.
* Multi-Domain Computer Vision Engine: Instant photo-based classification and 
  treatment generation for human skin, animal fur/lesions, and crop leaves.
* Geolocation Pharmacy Radar: Haversine formula calculation, live map routing, 
  and real-time order tracking (Order Placed ➔ Pharmacist Packed ➔ Rider on the Way).
* Offline-First Resilience: IndexedDB via Dexie.js for uninterrupted usage in 
  low-connectivity environments, syncing bidirectionally with Supabase PostgreSQL 
  upon network restoration.
